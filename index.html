The Development

A New Standard
of Luxury Living

Estancias del Bosque Reserve is a private boutique community of 22 modern luxury residences in Trujillo Alto, Puerto Rico. Each home is thoughtfully designed to blend seamlessly with the established tropical surroundings while delivering an uncompromising level of quality and craftsmanship.

Clean geometric lines, premium finishes, and lush landscaping create homes that are as beautiful from the street as they are from within. Located in a fully established neighborhood — not a remote development, but the heart of Trujillo Alto with every urban convenience minutes away.

Why Estancias del Bosque Reserve

Crafted for
Exceptional Living

Modern Architecture

Contemporary designs with clean geometric lines, warm travertine accents, and floor-to-ceiling windows that bring the lush outdoors in.

2-Car Covered Parking

Every residence includes a spacious covered carport designed to accommodate two vehicles while complementing the home's architectural aesthetic.

Tropical Landscaping

Lushly designed grounds featuring native palms, curated plantings, and manicured gardens that create a private tropical oasis around each home.

Premium Finishes

Imported materials, high-end fixtures, and custom millwork define interiors that exceed luxury standards — details you'll notice every single day.

Thoughtful Floor Plans

Three distinct models — two single-level designs and a two-story option, ranging from 3 to 4 bedrooms with 3.5 baths — each with flexible layouts that can be tailored to your lifestyle and needs.

Boutique Community

Only 22 private lots — an exclusive enclave with controlled access and a scale that ensures your community remains intimate, secure, and truly elevated.
