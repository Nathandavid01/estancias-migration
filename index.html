The Development

A New Standard
of Luxury Living

Estancias del Bosque is a private boutique community of 22 modern luxury residences in Trujillo Alto — a boutique product designed for those who want an exclusive, green enclave inside an established city, not a remote development.

Set within a fully established urbanización with complete infrastructure, each home blends into mature tropical surroundings while delivering craftsmanship on par with the finest private residences in Puerto Rico. The heart of Trujillo Alto, with every urban convenience minutes away.

Why Estancias del Bosque

Crafted for
Exceptional Living

Modern Architecture

Polished, colored-cement facades with generous eaves and clean geometric lines — a serious, natural, and sober aesthetic that ages beautifully.

Natural Light & Cross Ventilation

Homes designed to breathe — abundant natural light and cross ventilation through windows where applicable, for comfort that works with the climate.

Thick 7/16" Glass Windows

Premium, Move-In Ready

Thoughtful Floor Plans
